Watcher

A watcher is someone who doesn’t need eyes to see.
Why else would we have our senses?
To use tarot and the elements. duh! We are nothing but energy and matter moving through time, a concept where all we know is our past; we will never see the full picture so long as we reside here. Not until full completion of the life cycle on earth (ironically, the Tarot Card - The World, signifies completion…).
Tarot can make predictions based on past and current events. It also reflects energy. It’s important to know every person is different. Some are protected, some are not. This means some people are harder to read. Or someone’s spiritual aura could be stronger, and only their energy is appearing. You can always ask for a clarifying card.
A tarot reader can access untapped energy, makeing them an observer.

Go on, test tarot.

What is Tarot?

Tarot reflects the current state of being. Like the stock market, you can predict certain trends, but it is not all guaranteed. You need to use your intuition and knowledge of the cards to get a proper reading. You cannot go to every person to access your essence. The cards show you data; interpret it in order to follow or create a proper path for your queries and projects.

Remember, it's all about energy. Sometimes the tarot cards will be bad and not want to say anything or help you. It is important to respect the balance of give & take, so walk away. Rest.

Tarot Care

Like the Magician, we'll use our spiritual power to bring intention into reality.

Tarot is a form of divination you should be using for any occasion regarding your life; work, love, confusion, clarity.

Tarot is a tool, we all must use it appropriately.

Before a reading, cleanse your cards with sage spray or incense. Music is optional but chanting an intention is a must.

If you have magic oil, use it. Better yet, use it every time you read tarot cards.

You may get tired, so stay hydrated. Since you are accessing energy, make sure to relax afterward.

Do's & Don'ts

Do's
  • Cleanse frequently.
  • Keep them sealed so you don't lose individual cards.
  • Trust your intuition.
  • Ask for permission to read someone.
  • Keep a journal. Some readings may take time to make sense. Keep track of what's pressing in your subconscious.

Don'ts
  • Let strangers touch your deck. Energy is sacred.
  • Treat them carelessly.
  • Don't ask the same question multiple times.
  • Don't believe that fate is sealed by the cards.
  • Don't force the cards to give you answers.
  • Don't do a reading when you're not in a good mental state.
